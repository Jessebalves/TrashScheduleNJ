Welcome

Welcome to the TrashScheduleNJ Website! This website was originally developed and designed to help residents of Elizabeth, New Jersey to keep track of their trash schedules. We currently are based in Union County, New Jersey, which is why this website primarily focuses on supporting towns within the Union County area. We currently support Elizabeth and Kenilworth, New Jersey, and we are working on implementing other towns in Union County.

How to Use

To view your trash schedule for the current week, please head to the 'home' page in the navigation bar located above, and start typing your home address. Pick your address from the list that appears and your schedule is filled in. If your address is not in the list, you can type the house number, street name and town in yourself. If you'd like to see if your town is currently supported by this website, please read the section above. If you're interested in communicating with either of the two developer's who had a hand in creating this website, please visit 'Contact Us' to see contact information for the both of us.

Purpose of building

My partner and I first created this personal project to gain experience using Java, and that is the only programming language used in the original version of the Trash Schedule program. After completing the initial version of the project, my partner and I believed we could provide experience for ourselves and a service to the people of New Jersey if we converted the program into a website that people could visit. Our overall plan is to incorporate as many towns within the Union County area, and then eventually get to a point where we can begin incorporating other New Jersey towns in counties other than Union County.
